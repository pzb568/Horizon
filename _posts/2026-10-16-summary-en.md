### [Google auto-links phone numbers to all Android Google accounts](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-auto-phone-number-verification-android-3723117/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google has enabled automatic phone‑number verification on Android devices, linking the device’s phone number to every Google account signed in on the device—including primary, secondary, work, and other accounts—without showing a clear confirmation prompt. Users can disable the “auto‑verify phone number” toggle in Settings → Google services, but numbers already linked remain attached and must be removed manually from each account’s personal information page.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 16, 00:02

**「Phone‑Number Linking Prerequisite」** Google accounts on Android support optional phone‑number association, which is used for account recovery, two‑step verification, and syncing across services. Until now, users had to manually add or verify a number in their account settings, and the device’s phone number was only used when the user explicitly opted in to the verification flow.

**「Impact」** All users with multiple Google accounts on a device now have their phone number automatically attached to each account, requiring manual unlinking to protect privacy. Developers should expect the phone number to appear in account profile data by default, which may affect privacy‑compliance checks and UI designs that display user contact information.
